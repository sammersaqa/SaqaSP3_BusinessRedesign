Meet Luis Hernandez

In 2020, Luis Hernandez didn't just start a business — he planted a vision. Legacy Lawn Care & Landscaping began with one truck, one mower, and a promise: to treat every yard like it was his own.

Through grit, craftsmanship, and word-of-mouth trust, Luis built a reputation that now stretches across Stockton. From early morning mowings to full-scale landscape designs, every job reflects his commitment to quality, reliability, and personal pride.

Today, Legacy Lawn Care offers more than just services — it offers peace of mind. Whether you need weekly maintenance or a complete outdoor transformation, Luis and his team deliver results with care, consistency, and a local touch.
